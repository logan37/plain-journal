Plain Journal is a private, offline daily journal. Track your mood, meals, tags,
and period markers, then look back with a calendar and an insights dashboard.
Weather for a city you choose is included. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Everything you write stays on your device. Plain Journal has no sign-up, no
server of its own, and no analytics. Your data is deleted when you uninstall
the app.

FEATURES

* Calendar home - colour-coded dots per day for mood and period, so a month at
  a glance shows how you have been.
* Journal entries - free-form notes with a five-point mood scale, plus your own
  custom tags for things like sleep, exercise, or symptoms.
* Food and period tracking - optional per-day food notes and period markers you
  can switch on or off.
* Weather - current conditions and a seven-day forecast for a city you pick,
  powered by Open-Meteo (no API key, no account).
* Insights - streaks, mood distribution, a monthly mood trend, and your most
  used tags.
* Gender-aware - a one-time setting decides whether period tracking is shown;
  choose Female, Male, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.

PRIVACY

All entries are stored locally on your device. The only network request the app
makes is to Open-Meteo for the weather forecast of the city you choose. There
is no account and nothing is uploaded to a server we control.
